## Teaching Philosophy
My goal as a teacher is to help students become self-reliant musicians who know how to think, listen, and practice with purpose. Strong fundamentals matter, but they are always in service of expressive playing, personal confidence, and musical ownership.

Lessons emphasize sound development, technique, articulation, phrasing, vibrato, and the mental side of performance. I want students to leave each lesson with clear next steps and a stronger sense of trust in their own musical instincts.

I am especially interested in helping students experience flute playing as something creative and expansive. That includes curiosity about repertoire, interpretation, and influences beyond the standard path, including the ways Indian classical listening has informed my own musical thinking.

## Private Studio
My studio is designed for motivated students at many stages. Some are preparing region auditions, solo contests, college auditions, and advanced repertoire. Others are returning to the flute after time away or building a strong foundation for the first time.

Students can expect individualized lesson planning, collaborative repertoire choices, thoughtful practice strategies, and support for long-term growth rather than quick fixes. I also bring a strong understanding of Texas band culture, audition preparation, and the transition into collegiate-level study.

## Lesson Options
### 60-Minute Private Lesson
Best for advanced students, college-level players, and anyone who wants more time for deeper technical and musical work in one session.

### 30-Minute Private Lesson
A strong fit for beginners, younger students, and adults who want consistency and momentum in a shorter format.

### Audition Coaching
Focused work for region auditions, college auditions, competitions, recordings, or any high-stakes preparation that benefits from detailed feedback.
