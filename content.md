## About
Music has been a constant in my life from a young age, and flute performance remains the center of my work. My years of performing in professional orchestras, elite wind ensembles, intimate chamber groups, and as a concert soloist have shaped a career that is both rigorous and flexible.

Alongside performance, I care deeply about teaching. I work with students who want strong fundamentals, artistic confidence, and a clear sense of how to practice with purpose.

My artistic voice is shaped by both Western classical training and a deep interest in Indian classical influence, especially in the way it opens up listening, phrasing, sound, and musical imagination.
