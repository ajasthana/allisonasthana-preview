## About
Music has been a constant in my life from a young age, and flute performance remains the center of my work. My years of performing in professional orchestras, elite wind ensembles, intimate chamber groups, and as a concert soloist have shaped a career that is both rigorous and flexible.

Alongside performance, I care deeply about teaching. My goal is to help students become self-reliant, curious musicians who develop strong fundamentals, artistic confidence, and a sense of ownership over their musical growth.

I am especially drawn to repertoire and pedagogy shaped by cross-cultural listening. That includes a special interest in bansuri study and repertoire inspired by Hindustani music, which informs how I think about sound, phrasing, and musical imagination.
