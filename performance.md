## Current Activities
### Monarch Chamber Players
![Performance portrait](assets/images/hero.jpeg)

I serve as Executive Director and flutist with Monarch Chamber Players, shaping artistic direction, partnerships, audience engagement, and collaborative programming while continuing to perform as part of the ensemble.

My work with Monarch is grounded in building thoughtful, high-level chamber music experiences that feel both artistically rigorous and inviting to audiences.

### Emissary Quartet
![Teaching studio portrait](assets/images/teaching.JPG)

With Emissary Quartet, I work as both performer and educator. This includes chamber performance, teaching activity, and community-facing programming that reflects the ensemble’s versatility and clarity of purpose.

Emissary allows me to bring polished ensemble playing and meaningful educational work into the same artistic space.

### Orchestral Work
![Portrait image](assets/images/portrait.jpg)

I also perform with orchestras around the state, bringing a chamber musician’s attention to color, collaboration, and flexibility into larger ensemble settings.

This work complements my chamber and solo activity and keeps me engaged with a broad range of repertoire, conductors, and performance contexts.

## Performance Focus
My performance work moves fluidly between solo, chamber, and orchestral settings. Across all three, I am especially interested in programming that feels personal, communicative, and musically vivid.

I am drawn to repertoire by underrepresented composers, music shaped by cross-cultural influence, and performances that invite audiences into a genuine listening experience.
